<objective>
Settle and assert the unknown-SNI and no-SNI posture.

Purpose: F-04 recorded that an unknown or absent SNI is answered with the site
certificate. That is a concealment limitation, not a bypass, but the repository
cannot keep it as an unexamined default: an active prober that sends a random
SNI sees a site certificate instead of the destination's.
Output: a chosen posture, an assertion that encodes it, and a documented
decision. The baseline is measured first so the decision is made against
observed behaviour rather than against the prose.
</objective>
